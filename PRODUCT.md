# PRODUCT.md — Arcflow

Product context that design decisions hang on. Kept short.

## What it is
Arcflow is a team planning board in the class of Jira and Azure DevOps, but
sharper and more opinionated. Teams plan in Arcs, a cycle of a fixed number of
sprints, and work flows from Epics on the Arc Board down to Tasks on the Sprint
Board. It also has a private Daily checklist, a competitive Leaderboard,
Announcements, and a LeetPing activity feed.

## Audience
Small delivery teams, three to a few dozen people, who want a fast, focused board
without the weight of enterprise Jira. Technical users, comfortable with keyboard
driven tools.

## Brand and product lane
An engineering instrument. Precise, dense but calm, confident. Closer to Linear
than to Trello in polish, but with its own Arc based planning model. Not playful,
not corporate.

## Voice
Plain and direct. Name things by what the user controls. Buttons say what happens.
Errors are specific and never apologize. No filler, no marketing tone.

## Anti references
- Generic AI dashboard slop: purple gradients, card in card, everything boxed.
- Default shadcn look, Inter or system fonts.
- Toy like touches that undercut a serious tool, for example full screen weather
  particles over the work area.

## Signature
Spend boldness in one place per screen. The Sprint Board Kanban and the
Leaderboard podium are the two hero moments. Everything else stays quiet.
